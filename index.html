About me

Turning ideas into playable experiences

I'm a Unity developer and Computer Science graduate who has been building games professionally since 2020. I've shipped 2D and 3D games for mobile, desktop, web and gaming consoles.

Beyond traditional games, I build Augmented Reality apps with Vuforia, VR experiences on Meta Quest 2 & 3, and motion-controlled games with Kinect. I enjoy performance-critical work, like squeezing smooth gameplay out of WebGL builds that run inside mobile apps.

I've also led a small team as game designer and lead developer, so I care about clean code, clear communication and delivering on time.
